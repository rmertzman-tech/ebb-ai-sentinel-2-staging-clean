# SENTINEL-11A Operator Guide

Use the same two-commit procedure. Upload the ordinary `EBB-AI-SENTINEL-11A` folder to repo root and commit to `main`; then separately upload `sentinel11a-live-certification.yml` to `.github/workflows/` and commit. In Actions select **EBB AI SENTINEL-11A RC2.8-vs-RC2.9 Production-Compatible Live Certification**, verify `main` and endpoint `https://ucp-backend-4dig.onrender.com/api/claude-proxy`, and run exactly once. On success obtain `sentinel11a-separation-receipt` first; do not obtain custodian results until blind review is locked. Expected artifacts: `sentinel11a-blind-review`, `sentinel11a-custodian-results`, `sentinel11a-separation-receipt`. On failure, do not rerun automatically; preserve `sentinel11a-custodian-failure-diagnostics` and diagnose first.
